# Rodriguez Diaz et al., GLD (arXiv:2104.12345v2)

*Machine Learning-based Lie Detector applied to a Novel Annotated Game Dataset*  
Nuria Rodriguez Diaz, Decky Aspandi, Federico Sukno, Xavier Binefa. 2021.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.12345

## What we use

- **Statement windows**, not single-frame face labels. GLD annotates ~37-frame utterances; live they collapse a statement if >30% of frames fire. Truthsayer’s `UtteranceTracker` keeps the windowing and emits ΔBPM / gaze / VRMS / cue-load. It does **not** use their VGG lie head.
- **Cross-domain failure** of a static-face net (generalization 57% vs ~58% majority-class “always truth”; other-task test ~44%). ReAct therefore **never `flag_review`s on a face/motion-only cluster**.

## What we do not use

- VGG-16 + FC “P(lie)” (at or below majority class on unseen people).
- LBP/HOG/SIFT lie SVMs.
- RealSense depth (recorded, unused in their classifiers).
